On-target shots only, so goals and saves. Regular seasons, penalties,

Two stages, following the architecture Opta documents for xGOT. Stage
one is xG-lite, whose logit enters here as a single term carrying
distance, angle, body part and situation. Stage two adds where the
shot finished inside the goal frame, in metres, lateral from the
centre and height from the ground. Distance and angle are therefore
fitted once rather than twice.

Specification notes. The pre-shot logit enters with a coefficient of
1.214 (se 0.073), significantly steeper than 1.0 (z = 2.92, p = .003).
Among shots that reached the frame, goal probability responds more
sharply to pre-shot quality than the pre-shot scale itself, which is
what conditioning on being on target should do, it strips out the
off-target noise the pre-shot model has to carry. Situation dummies
here are read against that inherited scale, so sit_corner being
positive does not mean corners are good, stage one already carries
their penalty.

This architecture replaced a single-stage model that fitted distance
and angle directly alongside placement. It is better on
cross-validated log loss at all five seeds tested (mean .49020
against .49218), on held-out log loss with each of the three seasons
held out in turn, and on decile calibration (Hosmer-Lemeshow
chi-square 24.0 against 35.2 on eight degrees of freedom). It uses
one fewer parameter. Ridge penalisation was tested across a lambda
grid with nested cross-validation and gained .00001, so the model is
left unpenalised. Situation dummies are kept because without them
the model over-predicts corner goals, z = +3.25 in sample. A season
dummy was rejected, it cannot be estimated for a season outside the
training sample.

Remaining misfit. The deciles below still disagree beyond chance
(chi-square 24.0, p = .002), concentrated in the fourth and sixth
bins. Totals by situation are calibrated to within a tenth of a
goal, so the misfit is in the ordering of middling chances, not in
the level. Closing it would need information this feed does not
carry, above all where the goalkeeper was.
