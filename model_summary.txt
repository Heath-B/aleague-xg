Validation
  External. Against Sofascore's own published match xG across 652
  team-matches, r = .859, mean difference -0.022 xG per team-match,
  median absolute difference 0.240. The slope against their figure
  is 0.933, so this model compresses the extremes slightly.
  Within-situation. Calibrated in every situation. Corners across
  three seasons, 186.0 predicted against 186 actual, z = -0.00.
  Out of time. Trained on 2023/24 and 2024/25 and tested on
  2025/26, log loss 0.28471, AUC .7359, total xG 7.5 per cent above
  actual goals. That lean traces to a league-wide scoring dip,
  10.16, 11.22 and 9.77 per cent of shots scored by season, not to
  any one situation. A single-season fit on 2025/26 is unbiased, so
  for season-specific work use the per-season coefficients in
  xg_model_coefficients_by_season.json.

Methods tested and rejected
  Shooter skill, leave-one-out and shrunk towards the league mean.
  491 shooters with a median of 16 shots each, and held-out log
  loss was worse at every shrinkage level tried.
  Splines on distance, and further interactions among distance,
  angle and body part. Better in sample, worse held out.
  A season dummy. It cannot be estimated for a season outside the
  training sample and collapsed held-out discrimination to chance,
  AUC .483.
  Score state at the moment of the shot. The only candidate that
  improved cross-validated log loss, by .00018, which does not
  justify the term or the extra pipeline step.
  Ridge penalisation, a lambda grid with nested cross-validation.
  Best at lambda 30, gaining .00012 in cross-validated log loss
  while held-out AUC fell from .7359 to .7351. Left unpenalised.

What the model cannot see. The feed carries no defender,
goalkeeper or pressure information, which is the main gap between
this model and the commercial ones. Note also that penalties are
excluded here, so these figures are not directly comparable with
published models that include them. Penalties are easy to predict
and inflate both log loss reduction and AUC.
